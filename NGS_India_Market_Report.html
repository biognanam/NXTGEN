🔑 Key Insight for Lab Setup

India's NGS market is among the fastest-growing globally, driven by a massive cancer burden, rising awareness of rare diseases, consanguinity-linked genetic disorders, and strong government backing through the Genome India Project and Precision Medicine Mission. The demand is shifting from research to routine clinical diagnostics — making now the ideal time to launch affordable clinical NGS panels.

  • Major NGS hubs: Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai, Mumbai, Delhi, Pune
  • Over 200 genomic research centres already operating in India
  • 60% drop in NGS costs over the last 5 years — enabling affordable panels
  • India Government allocated ~$120 million toward precision medicine initiatives

Affordable NGS Lab Setup Strategy for India

Cost Optimization

1. Platform Selection

Choose cost-efficient sequencing platforms suited to throughput needs:

  • Illumina MiSeq — targeted panels, amplicon (low volume)
  • Illumina NextSeq 550/2000 — mid-throughput, WES, WGS
  • MGI DNBSEQ-G400 — 30–40% cheaper reagents vs Illumina
  • Oxford Nanopore MinION — portable, TB/infection, low cost

2. Start with High-Volume Panels

Launch with panels that generate recurring, high-frequency orders:

  • NIPT (Basic + Advanced) — continuous prenatal demand
  • Lung cancer EGFR/ALK/ROS1 panel — NSCLC is most common
  • BRCA1/2 hereditary panel — risk assessment demand
  • Thalassemia + SMA carrier screening — high in South India

3. Bioinformatics & Reporting

Invest in local bioinformatics pipelines to reduce per-report costs:

  • Use open-source pipelines: GATK, BWA, FreeBayes, VarScan
  • Build Indian population variant database (IndiGenomics)
  • Integrate ClinVar, COSMIC, OncoKB for clinical interpretation
  • Semi-automated report generation (reduces TAT + cost by 40%)

4. Cost Reduction Levers

Key operational strategies to price competitively:

  • Batch 8–24 samples per run to minimize per-sample reagent cost
  • Use custom capture panels instead of commercial kits (save 30–50%)
  • Partner with hospitals for direct sample flow (no middleman margins)
  • Leverage NABL accreditation for insurance billing
